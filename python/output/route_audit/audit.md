# Audit puncak rute RuteStrip

Generated by `python/tools/audit_summits.py`, `complete_to_summit.py`, `extend_to_summit.py`, `render_previews.py` and `build_audit_report.py` (new trails later: `find_new_trails.py`) from `dataset/routes.json` + `dataset/gpx/` (143 routes, full original GPX).

## Method and thresholds

- Intended summit: OSM `natural=peak|volcano` nodes within 4 km of the track (one Overpass union query per 15 routes). Matched on the route's mountain name after removing Gunung/Mt./Puncak/Bukit/Puthuk prefixes and aliasing spelling variants (Arjuna->Arjuno, Ceremai->Ciremai ...); exact single-summit names beat multi-mountain names ("Arjuno-Welirang"), real mapped nodes beat NGA-GNS name imports (often mislocated). Without a name match: highest peak within 2 km of the track's high point. Manual choices are in `python/tools/route_audit_overrides.json` (each with a reason).
- Peak elevation: OSM `ele`, else SRTM 30 m DEM at the node.
- **COMPLETE**: the full track passes within **250 m** (horizontal, point-to-segment) of the peak AND its max elevation is within **80 m** of the peak elevation. Track elevations from phones/watches are often 50-150 m off, so the vertical test also passes when the DEM at the closest track point is within 80 m of the DEM at the peak, provided the peak node's `ele` agrees with the DEM (|ele-DEM| <= 100 m, i.e. the node is well placed).
- **Summit-crater rule** (tuned): when the peak is a `natural=volcano` node whose `ele` is >= 50 m above the DEM at its position, the node sits over the summit crater rather than on the rim high point (Slamet, Sumbing, Raung). Such a route is COMPLETE when the track reaches the rim within 800 m of the node, its max elevation is within 80 m of the peak ele, and the DEM at its closest point is not lower than the node's DEM - 30 m. Completions on these peaks may end at any OSM rim node within 800 m whose DEM is >= max(node DEM - 30, peak ele - 120).
- **INCOMPLETE**: farther than 250 m (missing horizontal distance and height reported).
- **NOT_A_SUMMIT_ROUTE**: destination is not a peak (crater rim, waterfall, ridge/forest traverse); each justified below.
- **DATA_PROBLEM**: wrong area (>3 km from the listed trailhead) or GPX elevation broken (median |GPX-DEM| > 150 m) with an otherwise ambiguous result.
- Completion: original track from the trailhead up to its point nearest the peak (round trips that turned back become one way), extended by the shortest path on the OSM path graph (`highway=path|footway|track|steps|bridleway` + all ways of `route=hiking` relations) to the way node nearest the peak (<= 150 m from it), <= 3 km and climbing. OSM noding gaps <= 30 m are joined; nothing else is drawn. Fallback: an OSM hiking relation / named trail from the same trailhead (<= 1.5 km) that reaches the summit.
- Output tracks are resampled every 30 m with DEM elevation (SRTM 30 m, OpenTopoData; Open-Meteo 90 m fallback) and carry ODbL attribution in `<desc>` and `<link>`.

## Caveats

- Track elevations of the original (phone/watch) GPX files are often 50-150 m off; the vertical test therefore also uses DEM-vs-DEM at the closest track point when the peak node's ele agrees with the DEM there.
- SRTM 30 m smooths sharp summits: DEM summit heights are typically 10-60 m lower than the surveyed height, so new/replacement GPX max elevation can be below the official peak height even when the track reaches the summit marker.
- Peak nodes imported from NGA-GNS (source=NGA-GNS) are frequently mislocated by hundreds of metres; they are only used when no better node exists (e.g. Gunung Watupayon, Gunung Salak Dua).
- Summit access changes: Semeru (Mahameru) summit access and Anak Krakatau landings are frequently restricted by the park authorities; these routes are COMPLETE geometrically, but check current status before promoting them.
- OSM data reflects what volunteers mapped; a path present in OSM is not a guarantee that the trail is open or safe. New trails were filtered for access=no and names containing Nonaktif/ditutup/tutup/closed, and Merapi summit routes were skipped.
- Nothing was downloaded from community/third-party GPX sites; routes not completable from OSM are listed under 'Perlu GPX dari komunitas'.
